# Executive Summary — Sales Performance Analysis

## Objective
The objective of this analysis is to evaluate sales performance and customer behaviour using historical sales data, with the goal of identifying key trends and business insights.

## Key Findings
- Sales performance shows clear variation across product categories and regions.
- A small group of customers contributes a significant share of total revenue.
- Certain product categories demonstrate consistent growth, while others show stagnation or decline.

## Insights
- Revenue concentration highlights opportunities for targeted customer retention strategies.
- Regional performance differences suggest potential for focused marketing or operational improvements.
- Product-level trends can inform inventory and pricing decisions.

## Next Steps
- Deeper customer segmentation analysis
- Time-based trend analysis (monthly / yearly comparisons)
- Integration of additional data sources for richer insights
